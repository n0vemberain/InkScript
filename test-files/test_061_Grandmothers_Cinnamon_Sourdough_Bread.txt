Notes & Guide: Grandmothers Cinnamon Sourdough Bread

Ingredients / Preparation Steps:
• 500g artisanal flour
• 350ml warm water (approximately 80°F)
• 10g fine mineral salt
• 100g active sourdough starter

Method:
1. Combine water and flour in a stone bowl. Rest covered for 45 minutes.
2. Fold in starter and salt gently. Stretch every 30 minutes for 2 hours.
3. Shape into a round boule and proof overnight in the refrigerator.
4. Bake inside a preheated Dutch oven at 450°F for 35 minutes.
